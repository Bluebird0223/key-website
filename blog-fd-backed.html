FD-Backed Credit Cards: The Smartest Way to Build Your Credit Score

By Keyy Editorial Team • May 25, 2026

Getting your first credit card can be frustrating — especially when you’re rejected for not having a credit history in the first place. It’s a common challenge for students and young professionals just starting out.

That’s where FD-backed credit cards come in, offering a simple and secure way to enter the world of credit without the usual barriers.

What is an FD-Backed Credit Card?

An FD-backed credit card (also known as a secured credit card) is a type of credit card issued against a Fixed Deposit (FD) that you maintain with the issuing bank. Unlike regular credit cards, which are approved based on your income documents and credit history, FD-backed cards are secured — meaning your deposit acts as collateral for the credit limit.

How Does an FD-Backed Credit Card Work?

  1. You open a Fixed Deposit with a bank.
  2. The bank holds the FD as collateral/security.
  3. You get a credit card with a limit usually matching 75% to 90% of your FD amount.
  4. You use the card like a regular credit card for purchases.
  5. Your FD continues to earn interest in the background.
  6. Your timely bill payments are reported to credit bureaus, helping build or repair your CIBIL score.

Who Should Apply for an FD-Backed Credit Card?

An FD-backed credit card is ideal for anyone looking to start or rebuild their credit journey without facing strict approval criteria:

  • First-Time Users: Students and entry-level professionals with no credit history.
  • Self-Employed: Individuals without traditional salaried salary slips or documented IT returns.
  • Credit Rebuilders: Individuals with low CIBIL scores aiming to repair their credit history.
  • Repeatedly Rejected Applicants: Anyone who has faced repeated rejections from traditional card issuers.

Conclusion

FD-backed credit cards offer a low-risk, guaranteed method to build your credit score. Since the credit limit is backed by your own deposit, banks are happy to approve you instantly. By paying your bills in full and on time, you can quickly build a CIBIL score above 750, which will unlock premium unsecured credit cards and better loan opportunities in the future.
