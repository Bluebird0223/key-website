Best 5 UPI Credit Cards in India 2026

By Keyy Editorial Team • May 23, 2026

UPI payments have rapidly become the most popular digital payment method in India, used for everything from small daily purchases to high-value transactions. However, most users still rely on bank accounts for UPI payments, missing out on valuable rewards and benefits.

This is where UPI credit cards in India are transforming the way people spend. By linking a RuPay credit card to UPI apps, users can now earn cashback, reward points, and enjoy an interest-free credit period on every transaction.

If you’re looking for the best UPI credit cards in India for 2026, this guide will help you compare the top options based on rewards, fees, and overall benefits.

What is a UPI Credit Card?

A UPI credit card is a RuPay credit card linked to apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm, allowing you to make UPI payments directly from your credit limit instead of your bank account.

Key Benefits of UPI Credit Cards:

  • Up to 45 days of interest-free credit
  • Earn cashback & rewards on UPI payments
  • Track all expenses in one place
  • No need to carry physical cards or enter card details for every scan

Top 4 Best UPI Credit Cards in India (2026)

1. Axis Bank SuperMoney RuPay Credit Card

The Axis Bank SuperMoney RuPay Credit Card is a lifetime free card that provides high cashback on transactions done through the SuperMoney app, making it extremely cost-effective for daily UPI users.

  • Joining Fee: Rs. 0

  • Cashback: 3% cashback on transactions through the SuperMoney App

Things to Consider Before Choosing a UPI Credit Card

While UPI credit cards in India offer great rewards and convenience, it’s important to understand a few key factors before choosing the right one.

  • Limited Merchant Acceptance: Some small merchants may block credit card-based UPI payments to avoid paying Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) fees.
  • Reward Caps: Many cards place monthly limits on the maximum cashback or reward points you can earn from UPI transactions.
  • RuPay-only Support: Currently, only RuPay credit cards can be linked to UPI apps. Visa and Mastercard variants are not supported for UPI scanning.
  • Interest Charges: Just like regular credit cards, interest will accumulate if you fail to pay your full credit card bill before the due date.

Pro Tips to Maximize UPI Credit Card Rewards

To truly benefit from the best UPI credit cards in India, you need to use them strategically:

  • Use for Small Daily Transactions: Even small daily payments at tea stalls, grocery stores, and pharmacies add up to significant rewards over time.
  • Always Pay Your Bill on Time: Set up auto-debit to avoid missing payment deadlines, ensuring interest charges never eat into your cashback rewards.
  • Track Your Points: Monitor your reward balance inside your banking app or the Keyy app to redeem points before they expire.
  • Combine Multiple Cards: Use one card for utility payments, another for groceries, and a separate one for online purchases to optimize reward structures.

Conclusion

UPI credit cards are redefining digital payments in India by combining convenience, credit, and rewards into one seamless experience. If you’re already using UPI daily, switching to a UPI-enabled credit card is one of the smartest financial moves in 2026.

However, the real value lies in how you use your card. Choosing the right card and optimizing your spending can significantly increase your rewards. This is where smarter tools like Keyy come in—helping you track, optimize, and maximize every payment effortlessly.
