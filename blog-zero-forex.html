International Spending Made Smarter: Best Zero Forex Markup Credit Cards in India (2026)

By Keyy Editorial Team • May 21, 2026

Zero forex markup credit cards are a game-changer for frequent international travellers in India, online shoppers on global e-commerce sites, and anyone making regular foreign currency transactions. Unlike regular credit cards that charge 2% - 3.5% + GST in forex fees, these cards let you spend at true interbank exchange rates, saving you thousands on overseas purchases.

Whether it’s booking vacations abroad, paying for international subscriptions, or shopping online from foreign retailers, a zero forex markup credit card maximizes savings while offering premium travel perks like airport lounge access, reward points on international spends, complimentary insurance, and more.

Here’s our curated list of the best zero forex markup credit cards in India for 2026, carefully selected based on fees, rewards, travel benefits, and real value.

Conclusion

In conclusion, having multiple credit cards is not about spending more—it’s about spending smarter. Different cards offer different benefits like travel rewards, cashback, lounge access, shopping perks, or credit-building advantages. Using the right card for the right expense helps you save more and earn better value. The key is to choose cards wisely and manage them responsibly, so your wallet works for your lifestyle and financial goals.
