Complete Guide to Credit Cards with Lounge Access in India

Air travel in India has grown rapidly, and with it, the demand for credit cards with airport lounge access has increased like never before. Instead of waiting in crowded airports, today’s travellers want comfortable seating, peaceful workspaces, complimentary food and beverages, free Wi-Fi, and an overall premium experience. This shift in expectations has made airport lounges a key travel perk, which is why many people now actively search for the best credit cards for airport lounge access in India before choosing a card.

Below is a complete list of the best credit cards for free lounge access in India.

List of best Credit cards for domestic and international lounge benefits:

1. Axis Atlas Credit Card

Joining Fee: Rs. 5,000
Annual Fee: Rs. 5,000

Axis Atlas Credit Card is one of the best travel credit cards in India, offering up to 5% value back on travel-related spends and 2% value back on other eligible categories in the form of EDGE Miles. These accumulated EDGE Miles can be easily transferred to multiple airline and hotel loyalty program partners, making it an excellent option for frequent travellers.

  • International Lounge Visits: 4 for Silver Tier, 6 for Gold Tier and 12 for Platinum Tier Cardholders
  • Domestic Lounge Visits: 8 for Silver Tier, 12 for Gold Tier and 18 for Platinum Tier Cardholders

2. Axis Bank Horizon Credit Card

Joining fee: ₹3,000
Annual renewal fee: ₹3,000

Positioning it as a strong mid-premium option among travel credit cards in India.

When it comes to airport lounge access in India and abroad, the card offers excellent value:

  • International Airport Lounge Access: Enjoy 8 complimentary international lounge visits per year (2 visits per quarter), making it a suitable choice for travellers looking for credit cards with international lounge access.
  • Domestic Airport Lounge Access (India):
    Visa Signature variant: Up to 32 free domestic lounge visits annually (8 per quarter)
    Mastercard World variant: Up to 24 free domestic lounge visits per year (6 per quarter)

Axis Bank Horizon Credit Card is a popular travel credit card in India offering strong airport lounge access benefits and value-back in the form of EDGE Miles. Cardholders earn 5% value back on travel bookings and 2% on other spends, making it ideal for frequent travellers. EDGE Miles can be redeemed or transferred to multiple airline and hotel partners, making it a flexible lounge access credit card for domestic and international travel.

3. Ixigo AU Credit Card

Joining Fee: Nil
Annual Fee: Nil (Lifetime Free Credit Card)

This is a lifetime free credit card with airport lounge access in India, ideal for frequent train and air travellers.

Lounge Access Benefits:

  • 16 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year
  • 1 complimentary international airport lounge visit annually
  • 16 complimentary domestic railway lounge visits every year

4. IndusInd Bank Avios Visa Infinite Credit Card

Joining Fee: ₹40,000
Annual Fee: ₹10,000

Airport Lounge Access:

  • 2 complimentary international lounge visits per quarter with complimentary Priority Pass membership
  • 2 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per quarter in India

The IndusInd Bank Avios Visa Infinite Credit Card is positioned as a premium travel credit card in India and a strong contender among the best lounge access credit cards in India for frequent international travellers.

Unlike typical co-branded airline cards, it partners with both Qatar Airways and British Airways, allowing cardholders to earn Avios points that can be redeemed for flights, upgrades, and travel bookings.

Conclusion

In conclusion, having multiple credit cards can be a smart strategy for better money management when used responsibly. The right mix of credit cards helps you maximize rewards, earn cashback, enjoy travel benefits, improve your credit score, and manage different expenses more efficiently. Instead of relying on one card for everything, using multiple credit cards wisely allows you to unlock more value from your everyday spending. The key is to choose the best credit cards for your lifestyle, track payments on time, and use credit strategically for long-term financial growth.
