Meet the Founders Behind Keyy: Building the Future of Intelligent Payments

By Keyy Editorial Team • May 28, 2026

At Keyy, we believe every payment should pay you back.

Today, millions of people spend through UPI, debit cards, and credit cards every single day but most of them unknowingly lose value on every transaction. Wrong card choices, missed rewards, poor redemption strategies, and complicated reward systems have made spending inefficient.

Keyy is changing that.

Instead of asking users to change their spending habits, Keyy adds an intelligent layer on top of existing payment behavior helping users choose the right card, maximize rewards, redeem smarter, and turn everyday spending into measurable ROI.

No hacks. No unnecessary complexity. No financial jargon.

Just intelligent payments designed for modern consumers.

But behind this vision is a team of founders bringing together expertise from technology, finance, product, operations, and high-growth startups.

This is the story of the people building Keyy.

Meet the Founders Behind Keyy

Keyy was built by four founders who understood one simple truth: Payments are digital. But they are still not intelligent enough.

Each founder brings a different strength to the company from AI and engineering to finance, product strategy, growth, and operations.

Together, they are building one of the most ambitious intelligent payments platforms designed for the modern Indian consumer.

Aman Baheti

Co-founder & CEO
University of Huddersfield (Scholar) | 5+ years experience

Aman Baheti is a graduate of the University of Huddersfield, where he earned a 50% academic scholarship, awarded to only the top 10 students in a cohort of over 400. He also scored 680 on the GMAT Focus, a result that reflected his strong analytical and leadership potential. Despite having a pathway to leading global business schools, Aman chose to channel his ambitions into entrepreneurship and company building.

Over the past 5+ years, Aman has built and scaled startups across product, growth, marketing & operations. He previously co-founded a venture that was recognized among The Times of India's Top 10 Student-Led Startups and later joined NowZone as Head of Marketing & Strategy. There, he helped drive 50% month-on-month growth and played a key role in securing ₹15 crore in funding, gaining hands-on experience in scaling businesses, go-to-market execution, and growth strategy.

Naman Baheti

Co-founder & CFO
Chartered Accountant | ISB Graduate | IIM Indore | 5+ years experience

Naman Baheti is a Chartered Accountant, ISB alumnus, and holds an Investment Banking certification from IIM Indore. He scored 725 in the GMAT Focus and also secured pre-incubation support and a ₹12 lakh grant for Keyy at ISB.

He began his entrepreneurial journey early by co-founding a startup recognized among the Top 10 Student Startups in India by The Times of India Group. He was also runner-up at the regional round of the Global Student Entrepreneur Awards (GSEA). In a previous venture, he led strategic partnerships, including onboarding the CEO of Tata 1mg onto the platform and worked at a venture capital firm, where he recommended an investment in a startup that later delivered a 5x exit within two years.

Naman has 5+ years of experience in venture capital and startups with expertise in finance, fundraising and strategy.

Vedant Biyani

Co-founder & CTO
BITS Pilani | Built & exited startup (T-Series) | Apple | 10+ years experience

Vedant Biyani is a Computer Science graduate from BITS Pilani. His passion for building products started early when he founded a startup during his college years, which was later acquired by T-Series, demonstrating his entrepreneurial mindset and ability to turn ideas into successful businesses.

Over the past 10+ years, Vedant has built scalable, high-performance systems at leading global technology companies, including Apple, Starburst, and Infor. He brings deep expertise in AI, machine learning, large-scale system architecture & cloud infrastructure, having designed and deployed technology solutions that serve millions of users. His experience spans product engineering, data-driven systems, and enterprise-scale platforms, making him a key driver of technology innovation and execution.

Himanshu Shah

Co-founder & COO
Chartered Accountant | PwC India | Head of Operations | 5+ years experience

Himanshu Shah is a Chartered Accountant with 5+ years of experience across PwC India and as Head of operations - Finance at Artefact Projects (₹55+ crore market cap), bringing a strong foundation in building structured systems and managing scale. His expertise extends beyond finance into end-to-end business operations, including process design, cross-functional execution, partnerships, and organizational management. At Keyy, he leads operations as COO, driving seamless execution across functions and ensuring the company scales with strong operational discipline.

Building the Future of Intelligent Payments

The founders of Keyy believe users should not have to:

  • Memorize reward categories
  • Compare cards manually
  • Lose cashback unknowingly
  • Struggle with complicated redemption systems

Technology should do that work automatically.

That’s exactly what Keyy is building.

An ecosystem where:

  • Payments become smarter
  • Rewards become effortless
  • Spending becomes optimized
  • Financial decisions become intelligent in real time

All while keeping the user experience simple and seamless.

Because at Keyy, the belief is simple: Every payment should pay you back.
